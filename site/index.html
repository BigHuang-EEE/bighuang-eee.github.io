About me

Background & interests

I am pursuing a B.Eng. in Information Engineering at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), with graduation expected in June 2027. At SUSTech, I have worked with Prof. Xiaoying Tang and Prof. Jiankun Wang in the Department of Electronic and Electrical Engineering.

From January to May 2026, I was a visiting student at the University of Pennsylvania, where I worked with Prof. Enric Boix.

From June to August 2026, I was a visiting research student with Prof. Shiyu Zhao at Westlake University, developing real-world RL for aerial manipulation.

Research interests

  • Topological photonics & nonreciprocity
  • Photonic crystals & metamaterials
  • Synthetic gauge fields & duality
  • Time-varying & spatiotemporal photonics

I hope to extend this work toward integrated photonic platforms for reconfigurable control of light.
