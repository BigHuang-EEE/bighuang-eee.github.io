Research Experience

Undergraduate Research Assistant

2025-02 – present

Topological Physics Research Group, Department of EEE, SUSTech

Shenzhen, China

Supervisor: Prof. Zhen Gao. Pseudospin-locked chiral edge states in gyromagnetic photonic crystals; gauge-field-induced duality and Hofstadter photonic lattices; cavity-mediated topological transport in metallic dipole arrays.

  • Led full-wave modeling and sample design for a microwave gyromagnetic photonic crystal supporting pseudospin-locked chiral edge transport under broken time-reversal symmetry.
  • Identified QSH-like, QAH-like, and trivial phases through bulk and projected band structures, p-d band inversion, pseudospin-resolved eigenfields, and spin Chern numbers.
  • Designed measurement configurations and participated in VNA transmission and near-field mapping; measurements showed ~20 dB directional transmission contrast within the bandgap and pseudospin-selective excitation of the chiral edge mode.
  • Designed a pseudospin multiplexer/demultiplexer and a low-crosstalk waveguide crossing based on the coexistence of multiple topological phases.
  • Developed tight-binding and full-wave models of gauge-flux-engineered dual and self-dual photonic crystals; investigated Hofstadter-butterfly-like spectra via engineered coupling modulation.

Visiting Research Student

2026-01 – 2026-04

School of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, Nanyang Technological University (NTU)

Singapore

Supervisor: Prof. Baile Zhang. Terahertz chiral valley edge states in magneto-optic photonic crystals.

  • Designed and numerically validated a fabrication-compatible terahertz Chern-valley photonic-crystal interface supporting chiral, valley-selective edge modes.
  • Built 2D and 3D COMSOL bulk-band and supercell models to identify overlapping topological gaps and interface-localized modes.
  • Evaluated magnetic-bias configurations, fabrication tolerances, domain alignment, and finite air-layer effects, and re-optimized the structure for subsequent fabrication and experiments by NTU collaborators.
