Research & project experience

Physics-Consistent Safe RL for Aerial Manipulation

Jun. 2026 – Sep. 2026

Westlake University

Research Assistant · Advisor: Shiyu Zhao

  • Engineered the ROS/CLIK, Vicon, and MAVROS deployment stack with 8 Hz policy inference and 50 Hz rate-limited interpolation of base, arm, and gripper commands. Added deployment manifests, TorchScript export, and high-frequency logging for closed-loop evaluation and downstream fine-tuning.
  • Developed the Stage-III low-rank joint residual framework for real-world fine-tuning, using PreGME-conditioned context, feasibility-preserving latent composition on frozen nominal joint commands, correction-bank basis fitting, and causal replay. The adaptation does not alter base or gripper actions.
  • Addressed the arm sim-to-real gap by aligning Isaac Lab and onboard deployment contracts. Switched Stage-II control to measured-q joint-delta PPO, aligned commanded and measured joint observations, and validated one-step motion semantics in simulation and hardware.
  • Contributed as second author to a manuscript in preparation for ICRA 2027 on physics-consistent safe RL for aerial cart grasping and pushing.
