Research Interest

I am an undergraduate student with a strong foundation in Robotics, EEE and AI. My research interests span generative models, reinforcement learning, and robotics, with a focus on building efficient AI systems with stronger generalization capabilities. I am particularly excited about embodied AI and the challenge of enabling agents to perceive, reason, and act in complex environments. I aim to bridge principled mathematical thinking with practical system design, and look forward to contributing to research at the frontier of these areas.

Research & Project Experience

CA-LRR for Contact-Rich Aerial Manipulation

Research Assistant; Westlake University; Advisor: Shiyu ZhaoJun. 2026 - Sep. 2026

Contributed to implementing a low-rank residual RL framework for coordinated base–arm refinement around a frozen reference policy, using a five-dimensional SVD basis derived from successful simulation rollouts

Implemented controller-aware residual scaling based on tracking errors relative to prescribed bounds, with direction-preserving scaling to enforce action and joint limits

Engineered ROS-based hardware deployment integrating Vicon pose feedback, PreGME flight control, and reference and residual policy inference at 8 Hz

The resulting method increased mean task success from 44.91% to 56.72% across nine simulated load–slope conditions.

Hardware experiments demonstrated pushing a cart with a total mass of 35.54 kg and, in a separate test, pushing across a 2 cm-high uneven surface

LLM Hallucination Research
